Final Project Ideas

1. Barbershop Booking App
Customers choose a service, barber, date, and time, then view or cancel their bookings.
The apps checks the weather and holidays for the Chosen day so customers know what to 
expert.
    APIs
    - Open-Meteo: forecast for the appointment day
    - Nager.Date: public holidays, so those dates can be blocked or flagged

2. Recipe Finder
Search meals by ingredient or category, view the instructions, and save favorites. Each
recipe can also show nutrition details for its ingredients.
    APIs
    - TheMealDB: recipe, ingredients,categories
    - Open Food Facts: nutrition data

3. Workout Planner
Browse exercises by muscle group and build a weekly plan, with demo videos for each exercise.
    APIs
    - wger:exercises and muscle groups
    YouTube Data: demo videos

4. Travel Planner
Enter a country to see its basic info, the current weather, and the exchange rate 
for your country.
    APIs
    - REST Countries: country info, currency, flagged
    - Open-Meteo: weather
    - Frankfurter: exchange rates
